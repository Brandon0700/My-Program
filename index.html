Progression rule

When you reach the top of the rep range on every set with good technique, increase the load slightly next time (typically 2–2.5 kg for upper body and 2.5–5 kg for lower body). Keep around 1–3 reps in reserve on most working sets.

Training guidance

Compound lifts: rest 2–3 minutes. Isolation exercises: rest 60–90 seconds. Warm up progressively before heavy working sets. The deload week reduces working sets and load; do not chase personal records that week.
